Does McDonald's have a senior discount?

Yes, some McDonald's locations offer a senior discount, but there isn't a nationwide policy. This guide explains what it actually covers, what age qualifies, and when the app or McValue menu will save you more.

Last checked: August 5, 2026

Yes, some McDonald's locations offer a senior discount, but there isn't a nationwide policy. Because most McDonald's restaurants are independently owned franchises, each location decides whether to offer a discount and what it includes. That's why you might receive a discounted coffee at one McDonald's location but pay full price at another, even within the same city.

But knowing whether it exists isn't the whole story. Is the senior discount actually your best way to save money?

For many orders, the answer is no. Depending on what you're buying, the McDonald's app or McValue menu can save you more than a senior discount.

This guide explains when the senior discount is worth asking for, when another option gives you better value, and how to choose the best way to save before you place your order.

Why it's different at every McDonald's

Because almost every McDonald's is run by a different owner, not the corporate company.

About 90% of McDonald's restaurants are independently owned franchises. Each owner sets their own prices and decides which discounts to offer, including whether seniors get anything at all.

That's why one location might discount your coffee and the one across town won't. It's the same ownership structure deciding what the discount looks like, not a company-wide script.

Don't assume your regular spot's policy applies elsewhere, especially if you're traveling or trying somewhere new.

What you actually get

At participating locations, the senior discount usually applies only to a small coffee or fountain drink, not burgers, meals, or combo orders.

Where a senior discount exists, expect roughly 50 cents to a dollar off, sometimes less.

Some locations go further and build "senior" into the ordering system itself, as an actual size option next to small, medium, and large. Where that's true, you're not asking for a favor. You're ordering off the menu like anyone else, which is also why the discount shows up automatically instead of depending on whether the cashier remembers to apply it.

Either way, don't expect a discount on burgers, meals, or anything beyond a basic drink. Even participating locations stop there.

What age qualifies (and how to actually ask)

Usually 55. Some locations set it at 60 instead.

There's no single rule, so the honest answer is: it depends on the store you're in.

You usually don't need ID, and where "senior" is already a menu size, you don't need to ask for anything special either. You just order the senior coffee the way you'd order a small or medium.

If asking still feels awkward at a location where it isn't a menu item, you're not the first person to feel that way. Ask before you pay, not after. It's much harder to add a discount once the receipt has printed.

Quick takeaway: if you're 55 or older, it's worth asking before you order. The worst outcome is that the location doesn't participate, and it only takes a few seconds to check.

Is the senior discount actually your best deal?

The answer depends on what you're buying, not just your age.

A senior discount usually reduces the cost of one drink. App deals and McValue promotions reduce the cost of your entire order. That difference is why the best option changes depending on whether you're buying a coffee, breakfast, or a full meal.

Your orderBest optionWhy
Coffee onlyApp coffee deal (when active) or senior discountBoth usually save about the same, but app deals often apply to any size instead of just a small coffee.
Breakfast orderApp or McValue breakfast dealMeal deals reduce the cost of the sandwich and hash browns, while the senior discount usually only affects the drink.
Full mealApp or McValueMeal promotions can save several dollars. The senior discount normally doesn't apply to meals.
Occasional visitorMcValue menuYou get immediate savings without downloading an app or creating an account.
Frequent visitorMyMcDonald's RewardsPoints and rotating offers build up over time, creating bigger long-term savings than a coffee discount.

The comparison above highlights one simple pattern:

  • Coffee purchase: the senior discount can still be a good choice.
  • Meal purchase: the app or McValue almost always provides better value because the savings apply to the whole order, not just the drink.

For example, if you're only ordering a small coffee, the senior discount may be the simplest way to save. But if you're ordering a breakfast, lunch, or dinner meal with a drink, an app deal or McValue offer will usually reduce the total cost more than the senior discount.

Most locations also treat these as separate offers. If a senior discount is applied, you generally can't redeem an app deal during the same visit, so you'll usually need to choose the option that saves you more for that order.

Before your next visit

Check the app before you order. That single habit saves more than asking about a senior discount ever will, especially if you visit more than once in a while.

If you're at a new location, don't assume it matches your regular McDonald's, for the same ownership reasons covered earlier. Ask about the senior discount before you pay, not after, and don't be surprised if the answer is no.

Calling ahead takes thirty seconds and settles the question before you're standing at the counter. Use the McDonald's Restaurant Locator to find the number for wherever you're headed.

Here's the short version: if you're grabbing just a coffee, ask for the senior price. If you're ordering breakfast, lunch, or dinner, skip straight to the app or McValue menu instead.

Think of the senior discount as a coffee perk, not a general money-saving strategy. For meals, the app and McValue usually provide better value. Knowing which one fits your order matters more than simply knowing the senior discount exists.
