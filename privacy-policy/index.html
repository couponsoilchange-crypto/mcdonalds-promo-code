Privacy policy

This policy describes exactly what this site does, and doesn't do, with your data. It's short because the site currently collects very little.

What this site collects

McDonaldsCoupons.us does not use cookies, does not run analytics or tracking scripts, does not require or offer user accounts, and does not have a newsletter or email signup. Browsing this site does not create a profile, session, or stored record tied to you.

Contact form / email

If you email us through the contact page, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that email (such as your email address and message). We use it only to respond to you and don't share it with third parties.

Hosting

This site is hosted as a static website. Our hosting provider may log standard technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) as part of normal web server operation, which is outside our direct control and not used by us for tracking.

Third-party links

This site links to McDonald's official app and website. Once you leave this site, McDonald's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

Changes to this policy

This policy reflects the site as it currently operates. If we ever add analytics, advertising, a newsletter, or user accounts, we will update this page to describe that change before it goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
