Does McDonald's have a student discount? Here's what's actually true right now

Fact-checked against official McDonald's sources: August 2026. This guide checks the claim against dated, primary sources, not against how often it gets repeated.

Last checked: August 17, 2026
The basics

No, not currently, anywhere. Here's the proof.

There is no companywide McDonald's student discount right now, in the US, the UK, or Ireland. That's not a guess. It's checkable in about two minutes.

McDonald's Ireland's own website still hosts the full terms for a student offer, a discounted meal for a valid student ID, but the redemption window it names ran from September 2022 to January 2023. That's more than three years closed, with no notice on the page saying so. McDonald's UK's own website hosts a similar page for a different student offer, this one running October 2022 to August 2023, also expired, also with no notice.

As for the US: no McDonald's corporate source anywhere states a student discount percentage. Not 10%, not any number. It doesn't exist to find, which is a strange thing to say about a claim that shows up on dozens of pages.

If neither of those things is a company lying outright, how can both still be this misleading?

Why this keeps happening

Here's the mechanism, and it's simpler than it looks.

McDonald's runs student offers as time-limited campaigns through a third-party verification partner, not as a standing policy. That means every one of them was built to expire from day one. The problem is what happens after: nothing on McDonald's own website flags a campaign page as over. No banner, no takedown, no "this offer has ended." The page just sits there, live and indexed, looking exactly like a current one.

Content sites researching "does McDonald's have a student discount" find that still-live page, or find another site's article about it, and treat findability as proof of currency. They publish it in the present tense without checking the date against today. Once a few sites do that, later sites copy the claim itself, not the work of checking it, and confidence builds through repetition instead of verification.

That explains the UK and Ireland pages. It doesn't fully explain the US "10%" figure, since no McDonald's page for it seems to have ever existed. The most likely explanation: several real fast-food chains do offer a genuine 10% student discount, and somewhere in a "student discounts at restaurants" roundup, McDonald's got swept into that list by pattern, not by fact. Once published, it spread the same way everything else here did: copied forward, never checked against McDonald's directly.

The handful of sources that get this right share one habit the rest don't: they date-stamp their own claims and revisit them, instead of assuming something true once stays true.

Given that, what exactly is getting blurred together in this specific case?

Myth vs. fact

The three different claims, untangled

Three separate things get folded into one "does McDonald's have a student discount" story. They deserve to be told apart.

ClaimWhat it actually was or isCurrent status
UK & Ireland student offer A real promotion via a third-party student-verification partner, giving a discounted or free item with a qualifying purchase Expired (Ireland: Jan 2023, UK: Aug 2023). One source reports the partnership itself was suspended in 2025; unconfirmed as of this writing.
US "10% off" discount A specific figure repeated across many pages, with no McDonald's source ever found for it No evidence it was ever real. Likely absorbed from other chains' genuine 10% programs through repeated listicle copying.
Archways to Opportunity A real, current McDonald's tuition-assistance program Genuinely active, but it's an employee benefit for McDonald's staff, not a customer discount. Easy to confuse with the discount question by name alone.

None of these gets a student anything at the counter today. So is there anything that actually does?

Available now

What a student can actually use today

Nothing student-specific. But McDonald's does currently run general offers through its app, open to any customer, students included, with no ID or verification required at all:

  • McValue: a rotating two-item deal (recently structured as 2 items for $6), open to anyone who checks the app
  • Free Fries Friday: a free medium or large fries with any purchase of $1 or more, every Friday
  • A first-purchase offer for new app users, typically a free item with a small minimum order
  • MyMcDonald's Rewards: points on every purchase, redeemable for free food over time

None of it requires proving you're a student, because none of it is aimed at students specifically. It's just what's available to everyone who opens the app. Specific items and prices rotate and vary by location, so check the app directly rather than treating any of the above as fixed.

The honest move isn't to look for a student-only door. It's to use the app the same way any other customer would.

If a new "McDonald's student discount" claim shows up next year, how would anyone know whether it's real?

Verify it yourself

How to check a claim like this yourself

Two questions do almost all the work: where did this claim come from, and when was it last true?

Not "how many sites say this" and not "does this sound plausible." A claim repeated on fifty pages is still unverified if none of them ever checked the primary source, and a page that looks official can still be describing something that ended years ago, exactly what happened here, on McDonald's own website, for over three years. Find the original source, find the date on it, and treat anything without both as unconfirmed, no matter how confidently it's written.
