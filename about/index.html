About McDonaldsCoupons.us

Who runs this site, what it covers, and how it decides what to publish.

What this site is

McDonaldsCoupons.us is an independent, informational guide to how discounts, deals, and offers actually work at McDonald's in the US. We write about the app's Deals tab, MyMcDonald's Rewards, receipt survey vouchers, McValue offers, and location-specific promotions like the senior discount.

We are not McDonald's. We don't process orders, issue offers, or control what's available at any restaurant. Every discount described on this site comes from McDonald's own systems, not from us.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish typed-in promo codes, because McDonald's doesn't use them in the US.
  • We don't guarantee that any offer described here is currently live at your location, since availability varies by franchise.
  • We don't use McDonald's logos, official photography, or trademarked visual branding on this site.

How we approach content

Every guide is checked against McDonald's own published information and app behavior, with a visible "last checked" date so you know how current it is. Where a claim depends on your specific restaurant, we say so instead of stating it as a universal fact.

If something on this site is outdated or looks wrong, the app's Deals tab is always the final authority, not this site.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by McDonald's Corporation or its franchisees. See our disclaimer for full details.
